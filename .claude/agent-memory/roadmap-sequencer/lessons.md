
## frontend-uplift-2026q2 (2026-05-20)
- **MoSCoW cut heuristic — enabler epics are natural Musts:** When a roadmap has a dependency fan-out where all value epics (e2, e3, e4) depend on a single ENABLER epic (e1), that ENABLER is the only Must. The value epics are Shoulds/Coulds — they can slip without breaking the OKR. This kept Must at 25% (well under 60%) and avoided the "flat-listed Must" anti-pattern.
- **RICE Confidence=50% on rollup epics even when candidate-level evidence exists:** The 26-candidate pipeline provided high-quality per-candidate RICE scores, but the epic-level rollup (aggregating multiple candidates into one T-shirt-sized epic) remained an estimate. Applied 50% at the epic level with an explicit asterisk — do not silently promote to 80% just because downstream candidates have evidence.
- **Milestone heading format inside the Now lane:** Phase prompt specified `### <slug>-mN — Title` (H3, three hashes) for Now-lane milestones inside the epic block. This is different from the default template which uses H4 (`####`) for epics. Confirmed: milestone headings are H3 inside the Now lane; story blocks nest under those milestones without additional heading.
- **Bundle-gate stories are their own slice:** For library-adoption stories where a size constraint exists (UPL-1, 200 KB ceiling), the pre-install measurement and post-install verification are separate XS stories (s3 and s5) sandwiching the installation story (s4). This makes the gate explicit and prevents the implementer from skipping the measurement under time pressure.
- **Spike for [SHOULD] assumptions when they block a high-value next-lane epic:** UPL-2's `[data-leaving]` a11y question was a [SHOULD] assumption, but because it gates the entire Next-lane e2 milestone, it warranted a ≤2-day spike. Do not skip spikes for [SHOULD] assumptions when the assumption's failure would block a Should-tagged epic entirely.
